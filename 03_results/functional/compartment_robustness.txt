Compartment-specificity robustness check
==============================================================
Leading-edge set: 49 splicing factors concordantly down-regulated in OA cartilage and OP BM-MSC.

Question: is the same coordinated down-regulation present in the
haematopoietic compartment? Tested per monocyte cohort and jointly.

  GSE56815   down  22/ 49 (44.9%)  binomial P = 0.5682
  GSE7158    down  27/ 49 (55.1%)  binomial P = 0.5682

  Concordant across BOTH monocyte cohorts: 11/49 (22.4%); expected 25% under independence; P = 0.7445

Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------
Neither monocyte cohort shows the coordinated down-regulation seen in
the mesenchymal compartment. Individual splicing factors do move in
monocytes, but not as a concordant programme, supporting the
compartment-specific interpretation.
